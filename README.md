# Morning Blessings Creator

Create beautiful morning blessing cards and share them daily with your community.

## Features

- 🎨 7 unique blessing styles
- 🎯 Personalize with your name
- 🌈 6 color themes to choose from
- 📥 Download as PNG image
- 📱 Works on mobile and desktop

## Blessing Styles

- ☀️ Cheerful & Bright
- 🌹 Romantic & Elegant
- 🦋 Playful Cartoon
- ✨ Sketch Art
- ☕ Coffee Morning
- ✍️ Calligraphy
- 🙏 Jehovah's Witness

## How to Use

1. Click the Settings icon (⚙️)
2. Enter your name (optional)
3. Choose a color theme
4. Select a blessing style
5. Click "Generate New" for different messages
6. Click "Download PNG" to save
